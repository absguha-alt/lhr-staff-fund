Scope 4 — In Scope ⚠ Analysis Required
LHR Compass Changes — Configuration & New Builds
LHR is a ring-fenced on-premise Compass instance for Liberty staff only. Staff benefits differ from market products (LCB/LCU) — staff elect individual GLA multiples, contribution rates and investment portfolios. Compass changes are required but detailed specifications are subject to analysis against the Fund Rules.
Known requirements from Fund Rules
Rule 4.1
Member contribution rate engine: min 1%, multiples of 1%, max 7.5%, 30-day notice to Employer & Fund; legacy 2.5% / 3.5% / 7.5% members moved to nearest 1% multiple on first change (Rule 4.1.3)
Rule 4.2
Employer contribution scale per member: 0, 1, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 7.5% of Fund Salary. Employer also funds admin fee and Fund Expense Contingency Account contribution (Rule 4.2.2)
Rule 13.2
Member Share Account: unitised per member across elected portfolios. Member Share = units × unit price. Vested vs non-vested split must be correctly calculated and exposed to ODS/SSP
Rule 15.13
Member investment choice: Compass must hold elected portfolio per member, apply default portfolio where no election is made, and record all switches as written instructions from the Administrator
Rule 7
Normal retirement age 60; voluntary early retirement from age 55 with employer consent. Up to 1/3 of Member Share as cash; balance purchases compulsory annuity. Compass must correctly calculate retirement benefit values for projection output
Rule 9 / s37C
Death benefit = Member Share lump sum distributed to dependants/nominees per s37C. Compass must hold current, up-to-date beneficiary nominations; ODS write-back path for SSP-initiated updates to be designed
Pending analysis
Flexible GLA election per member (2x–8x, approved vs unapproved, no split) — existing Compass capability or new build?
Annual review window enforcement — when elections can change (salary review, life events); how Compass gates these
Vesting scale configuration for savings portion — applicable rules and percentages require analysis
Rate change notification path back to SBG SAP when member changes contribution rate in Compass — two-way dependency
